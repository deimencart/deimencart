### 📖 About Me

I’m a mechatronics engineer from UNAM (Mexico) currently completing my Master’s in Robotics at Universidad de Zaragoza (Spain).

My background is hands-on. I started in the workshop: designing CAD mechanisms, soldering boards, and building physical prototypes—including an autonomous beach-cleaning rover for my undergraduate thesis and mobile robotics projects at the UNAM robotics club (CROFI).

Along the way, my focus shifted toward the software and physics behind complex physical interaction. Today, I work on one of the most challenging areas in manipulation: **deformable objects (cloth and textiles)**. For my Master's thesis, I simulate and control Franka Emika Panda and UR10e arms in **Genesis World**, leveraging GPU-accelerated physics (PBD/FEM) and computer vision to plan reliable manipulation trajectories.

In short: I like taking difficult physical interaction problems, modeling them accurately in simulation, and translating them into robust robot behavior.

---

### 🔬 Current Focus & Thesis Research

- **Deformable Object Manipulation (DOM):** Developing autonomous manipulation and folding pipelines for non-rigid materials (cloth) using Franka Emika Panda and UR10e arms.
- **Physics Simulation (Genesis World):** Simulating dynamic interactions with Position Based Dynamics (PBD) and FEM solvers on parallel GPU environments.
- **Field Robotics & Outdoor Navigation (UGV Voyager 3):** ROS 2 navigation architecture on an Ackermann-steered UGV. MAVLink/MAVROS 2 bridging on Raspberry Pi 3B + Pixhawk, 360° LiDAR scanning, and non-holonomic reactive navigation (Nearness Diagram).
- **Robot Perception & Active Vision:** Leveraging differential surface geometry (Monge patch, curvature analysis), feature matching (LightGlue, DINOv2), and RGB-D cameras to handle occlusions and wrinkles.
- **Kinematics & Control:** Cartesian impedance control, joint velocity tracking, and $C^1$ smoothstep waypoint interpolation.

### 🚀 Featured Projects

- 🧵 **Deformable Object Manipulation in GPU Simulation (Master's Thesis)**  
  End-to-end simulation and manipulation pipeline for textiles using Genesis World, Franka Emika Panda, and UR10e. Addresses challenges in multi-layer fabric grasping and active camera positioning.
  
- 🚙 **UGV Voyager 3 — Autonomous Rover Navigation (ROS 2 & ArduRover)**  
  Field-ready Unmanned Ground Vehicle with Ackermann kinematics (H2020 RAWFIE platform). Architecture includes Pixhawk (ArduRover) low-level control, Raspberry Pi mission computing, 360° LightWare SF40/C LiDAR, GNSS, and a custom Nearness Diagram reactive planner in ROS 2.
  
- 🏖️ **Autonomous Beach Cleaner Rover (B.S. Thesis — UNAM)**  
  Designed, fabricated, and tested an autonomous mobile robot tailored for granular terrain navigation and waste collection. Covered full mechanical architecture, actuator sizing, and low-level motor control.

- 🤖 **CROFI Robotics Portfolio (UNAM)**  
  Autonomous mobile platforms, Bluetooth-controlled motor drivers (DRV8833), and sensor integration developed during my time at the Faculty of Engineering robotics club.
